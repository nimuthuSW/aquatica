What you can do

Small actions, big currents

🏠 At home
  • Fix dripping taps — one drip per second wastes over 10,000 L a year.
  • Never pour oil, paint, medicines or chemicals down the drain.
  • Run washing machines and dishwashers only when full.
  • Choose phosphate-free, biodegradable cleaning products.
🛒 When shopping
  • Carry a reusable bottle, bag and coffee cup.
  • Avoid products with microbeads and glitter made of plastic.
  • Choose certified sustainable seafood — or eat less of it.
  • Buy clothes made from natural fibres; synthetic fabrics shed microfibres in the wash.
🏖️ At the beach & river
  • Leave nothing behind — and pick up three pieces of litter you find.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen without oxybenzone and octinoxate.
  • Never touch or stand on coral; keep your distance from wildlife.
  • Join a local clean-up — or start one!
📣 In your community
  • Ask your local council about wastewater treatment and storm drain filters.
  • Support marine protected areas and river restoration plans.
  • Share what you learn — awareness spreads like ripples.
  • Vote for leaders who prioritise clean water.
