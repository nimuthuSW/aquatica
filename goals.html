Roadmap

The descent to 2030

Our submarine dives deeper with every milestone.

  1. 2024

    Launch source-to-sea programme ✅

    First river interceptors installed and coral nurseries established at three sites.

  2. 2025

    10,000 volunteers ✅

    Our volunteer network passed 10,000 people across five continents.

  3. 2026

    Open water data platform 🔄

    Publishing real-time groundwater and river-quality data for anyone to use.

  4. 2027

    50 communities with safe water

    Expanding Safe Water for Schools to 50 communities with local water committees.

  5. 2028

    1,000 tonnes of waste intercepted

    Scaling river interceptors to stop plastic before it reaches the sea.

  6. 2029

    Community marine reserves

    Helping coastal communities establish and manage 10 locally led marine protected areas.

  7. 2030

    Net-positive water impact 🎯

    Every region we work in has more clean water and healthier marine life than when we arrived.
