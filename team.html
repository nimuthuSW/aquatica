Regional presence

Our four field hubs

Local coordinators, dive boats and water-testing labs anchored in the regions we serve.

West Africa Hub

📍 Lagos & Accra

Community borehole engineering, school hygiene programmes and coastal turtle patrols.

Coral Triangle Hub

📍 Raja Ampat & Klang

Thermal-tolerant coral nurseries, dive cooperative training and river plastic interceptors.

South Asia Hub

📍 Chennai & Sundarbans

Mangrove hydrology restoration, rainwater aquifer recharge and acoustic well telemetry.

Americas Hub

📍 Osa Peninsula & Bogotá

Nesting-beach dark-sky ordinances, open hydrological data and watershed education.
